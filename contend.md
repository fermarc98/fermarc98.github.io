## Selected Projects

### GenerativeMPC

VLM-RAG-guided whole-body MPC framework with virtual impedance for bimanual mobile manipulation. The system links semantic reasoning to compliant physical interaction and uses retrieved task-relevant episodes to configure impedance gains, velocity limits, and safety margins.

### HapticVLA

Isaac Sim digital twin and synthetic data-generation pipeline for tactile-aware vision-language-action learning. The project enables contact-rich manipulation of fragile and deformable objects without tactile sensing at inference time.

### SafeHumanoid

VLM-RAG-driven impedance-control system for humanoid robots, accepted at ACM/IEEE HRI 2026.

### Unitree H1 Cartesian MPC

Real-time Cartesian MPC for Unitree H1 humanoid bimanual manipulation, with control latency under 10 ms, hardware-in-the-loop testing, and sim-to-real calibration.

### Humanoid Dance Motion Imitation via RL

Isaac Lab reinforcement-learning pipeline for Unitree H1 motion imitation using custom rewards and curriculum learning.
