Welcome

Marcelino Fernando

Robotics software engineer working on Physical AI — bringing robot foundation models and vision-language-action systems from simulation to safe, contact-rich deployment on bimanual and humanoid robots.

View Projects Download CV

My work centers on vision-language-action systems, humanoid and bimanual manipulation, and the compliant control that lets them act safely on real hardware. I build the learning, perception, and control stacks that turn language- and vision-derived intent into safe, contact-rich physical interaction.
